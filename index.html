About these numbers

The page asks Open-Meteo for fresh numbers every time you open it or press Refresh. Snowfall is modeled, not resort-reported: it comes from Open-Meteo's weather models, downscaled to each mountain's forecast elevation and summed hour by hour, so it can differ from what a resort measures on its stake. "Since Oct 1" adds Open-Meteo's daily archive through yesterday to today's hourly snowfall. Depth is the model's snow depth at that point. Stake cam images are served by OpenSnow.
